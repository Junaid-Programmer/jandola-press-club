Privacy Policy for Jandola Press Club posting tool

This app is used only by Jandola Press Club operators.

Data we use: TikTok Login Kit user.info.basic (account name and avatar) and a video.publish token so we can post videos the operator queued. Tokens stay on our private server. We do not sell data, show ads, or collect data from the public.

Videos: we only upload content the operator has the right to post.

Contact: the email used for the Jandola Press Club GitHub organization account.
